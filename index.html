What Is ROOTED

This isn't a farm brand.
It's a dignity brand.

There are millions of people who work the land — or whose families did, or whose families still do. They feed the country. They fix the fence. They get up before the sun and they don't ask for credit.

And almost nobody makes clothing that speaks to them without turning them into a costume.

ROOTED is for the woman who can fix a fence and still smells like cashmere. For the man who'd rather be in the barn than in a boardroom. For anyone who's tired of being treated like a punchline or a postcard.

The people who do the work are the point. Not the equipment. Not the aesthetic. You.

The Gold Edition

Fifty pieces per colorway. Solid gold aglets — the tips on the drawstrings — hand-stamped with the ROOTED mark. Each hoodie comes with a certificate listing the gold weight.

Wear it out. Send it back. We recover the gold, build you a new one, and ship it home. No charge. No registration. Whoever holds the garment holds the promise.

It doesn't matter if you bought it new, bought it secondhand, or were given it. The garment carries the warranty. A ROOTED Gold Edition never becomes trash.
